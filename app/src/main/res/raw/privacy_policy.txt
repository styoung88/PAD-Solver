PAD Solver Privacy Policy
Updated October 16, 2026

PAD Solver processes board images on your device to recognize orbs and calculate routes. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network uploads.

Selected images
You can choose an image with the system photo picker. PAD Solver reads only the image you select and does not require access to your photo library. Original images are not modified or deleted.

Screen sharing and overlays
Capture uses Android's screen-sharing consent to read a frame when you request a board capture. Stop sharing through Android's controls or close the overlay. Draw-over-apps access displays a floating toolbar and route; it does not automate game input. The app examines launcher app labels to find Puzzles & Dragons locally.

Storage, backup, and deletion
The most recent screen capture is stored in private app storage and replaced by the next successful capture. Selected images are read from their existing location. Board and route results are held in memory. Preferences are stored locally; solve preferences can be included in Android backup and device transfer. Captures and overlay preferences are excluded from backup. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove its local data. Images in your photo library and existing system backups are managed separately through Android and your backup provider.

Sharing and security
PAD Solver does not send images, board data, app lists, or preferences to the developer or third parties. Android permissions and private app storage protect access. Android and Google Play may process information independently under their own policies.
