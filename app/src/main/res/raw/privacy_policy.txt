PAD Solver Privacy Policy
Updated October 7, 2026

PAD Solver processes board images on your device to recognize orbs and calculate routes. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network uploads.

Images and photo access
You can choose an image with the system photo picker. If you enable automatic screenshots and grant full photo access, PAD Solver queries image names and folders to detect newly saved screenshots while the floating overlay is open. It reads matching screenshots to solve their boards. Screenshots can include other information visible on your screen. Turn off Enable screenshots or close the overlay to stop monitoring. Revoke photo access in Android Settings at any time. Original photos and screenshots are not modified or deleted.

Screen sharing and overlays
Capture uses Android's screen-sharing consent to read a frame when you request a board capture. Stop sharing through Android's controls or close the overlay. Draw-over-apps access displays a floating toolbar and route; it does not automate game input. The app examines launcher app labels to find Puzzles & Dragons locally.

Storage, backup, and deletion
The most recent screen capture is stored in private app storage and replaced by the next successful capture. Picked and automatically detected screenshots are read from their existing location. Board and route results are held in memory. Preferences are stored locally; solve preferences can be included in Android backup and device transfer. Captures and capture preferences are excluded from backup. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove its local data. Screenshots in your photo library and existing system backups are managed separately through Android and your backup provider.

Sharing and security
PAD Solver does not send images, board data, app lists, or preferences to the developer or third parties. Android permissions and private app storage protect access. Android and Google Play may process information independently under their own policies.

Contact
A public developer privacy contact will be added before distribution. This local preparation build is not ready for Play submission until that contact and a public policy URL are configured.
